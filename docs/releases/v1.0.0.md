Wedge keeps your Now in the menu bar. Everything else waits in Up Next. Finish, press Done, and the next item rises.

This is the first public version, rebuilt for macOS 26 Tahoe or later (Apple silicon and Intel).

## What's in 1.0

- **Now in the menu bar.** The top of your list is always in view. When the list is empty, Wedge shows its icon.
- **A panel that drops from the menu bar.** Add to Up Next, drag to reorder, click to edit. Drag a row to the top to make it Now.
- **Done.** Click the ring, press ⌘↩, ⌥-click the menu bar, or press ⌃⌥D from anywhere. Changed your mind? ⌘Z undoes it.
- **Shortcuts and Spotlight.** Add to Up Next, Mark Done and Get Now work from the Shortcuts app and Spotlight.
- **Settings.** Bold or colored title, your own Done sound (up to 3 seconds), the two global shortcuts, and Open at login.
- **Private by design.** No network access, no accounts, no analytics. Your list stays on your Mac.

## Install

1. Download **Wedge-{{VERSION}}.dmg** below and open it.
2. Drag **Wedge** onto the **Applications** folder.
3. Open Wedge from Applications.

### The first time you open it

This build is not yet signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS blocks it the first time:

1. When macOS says it can't verify Wedge, click **Done**.
2. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and scroll down to **Security**.
3. Next to "Wedge was blocked to protect your Mac", click **Open Anyway** and confirm.

You only need to do this once.
